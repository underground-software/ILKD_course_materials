## Class recordings

Unless otherwise specified, each session of class will be recorded.

Each recording will be password protected using the open source [`age` encryption](https://age-encryption.org/) program.

The password will be shared in class for the students who attended the lecture or on a case by case basis for students who need access under extenuating circumstances.

Links to the recordings will be shared on the course Matrix as they are ready.

You download a recording unlock it with `age` using the password and then play it with any video player of your choosing (e.g. [`vlc`](https://www.videolan.org/)).
