## Course Credentials

Students will be provided a set of credentials at the start of the course.
To obtain yours, navigate to the [registration](/register) page
and submit your student ID to receive a newly generated password with your username.

**Be sure to save these since they will only be displayed once**

If you lose or forget your credentials, get in touch immediately and we will reset your password as soon as we can.
Not being able to access your course account or the Matrix server is not a valid excuse for failing to complete coursework.
